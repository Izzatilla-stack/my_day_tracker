MY DAY TRACKER

This is a small offline-first phone schedule tracker made for your routine.

Features:
- Automatically chooses the correct schedule by weekday.
- Monday/Wednesday/Friday: gym after work + Italian class.
- Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday: driving lesson + split sleep + gym before work.
- Sunday: recovery day.
- Check off tasks.
- Progress is saved locally on the device.
- Week overview and 7-day stats.
- Works offline after first load when installed as a PWA.

HOW TO USE ON ANDROID
1. Put these files on any simple static web host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, etc.).
2. Open the hosted link in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Chrome menu -> Add to Home screen / Install app.
4. Open "My Day" from your home screen.

No account and no server/database are required. Your checkmarks are stored only in your phone browser.
